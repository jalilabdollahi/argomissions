In argocd-notifications-cm you need:
  service.webhook.<name>   the receiver's URL (in-cluster DNS name of the Service)
  template.<name>          what to send (a `webhook:` block with method, path and body)
  trigger.<name>           when to send (a condition on the app's operationState) and which templates
The Application is then subscribed with an annotation `notifications.argoproj.io/subscribe.<trigger>.<service>`.
