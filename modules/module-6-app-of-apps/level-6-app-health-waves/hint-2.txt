A sync wave only waits for the previous wave's resources to be Healthy. The root's resources are the two child
Applications, and `argocd app get beacon-root` shows no health for them at all.
Argo CD can be taught how to compute the health of any resource kind, including its own: look at the
`resource.customizations.health.<group>_<kind>` keys of ConfigMap argocd-cm in the Argo CD operator manual.
