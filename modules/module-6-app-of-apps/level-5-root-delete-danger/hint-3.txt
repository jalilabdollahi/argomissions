Restore apps/api.yaml (`git revert <commit>` or `git show <commit>^:apps/api.yaml > apps/api.yaml`) and add to every
child Application:
  metadata:
    annotations:
      argocd.argoproj.io/sync-options: Prune=false,Delete=false
`Prune=false` stops the root from pruning the child when its manifest is removed; `Delete=false` keeps the child when
the root itself is deleted. Push and refresh the root.
