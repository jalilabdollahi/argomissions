Make the key Argo CD reads equal to the hook's secret:
  kubectl -n argocd get secret argocd-secret -o jsonpath='{.data.webhook\.gitea\.secret}'
  kubectl -n argocd patch secret argocd-secret --type merge -p '{"data":{"webhook.gogs.secret":"<that base64 value>"}}'
Argo CD re-reads argocd-secret by itself (no restart). Re-send a delivery from Gitea (the hook's "Test Delivery" button)
to check. Re-creating the hook in Gitea with Argo CD's value as its secret is also consistent.
