Two ways, both fine:
  * set `spec.syncPolicy.preserveResourcesOnDeletion: true` on `kiosk-apps`, wait until the Applications lose the
    finalizer, then delete the ApplicationSet and apply `kiosk-fleet`;
  * `kubectl delete applicationset kiosk-apps --cascade=orphan` and apply `kiosk-fleet`, which adopts the apps.
Compare the Deployment UIDs afterwards.
