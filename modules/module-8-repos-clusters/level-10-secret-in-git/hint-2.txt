Three separate problems: (1) the manifest must leave Git (history stays - do not rewrite it);
(2) the password is burnt and must be replaced by a new one that never touches Git;
(3) the Secret must live in the cluster without Argo CD owning it, and pods must pick up the new value.
Argo CD will not prune on its own: after the commit the Secret shows as pending deletion. Environment variables
are read when a container starts.
