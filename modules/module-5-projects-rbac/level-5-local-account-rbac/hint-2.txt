Authentication (the account in argocd-cm) and authorization (argocd-rbac-cm) are separate. The RBAC ConfigMap is
empty, so a signed-in ci-bot can do nothing. Policy lines look like
  p, <subject-or-role>, <resource>, <action>, <project>/<application>, allow
Resource is `applications`; actions needed are `get` and `sync`; the object pattern limits it to project `shop`.
Roles are assigned with `g, <account>, <role>`.
