The ApplicationSet controller owns the spec of every Application it generates and rewrites it from the template
whenever something changes. Your change is "drift" from the template's point of view. There are two ways to
cooperate with it: tell the ApplicationSet which fields it must not overwrite, or make the field come from the
template/generator so that the template itself says `hotfix-1.8.1` for prod.
`kubectl explain applicationset.spec.ignoreApplicationDifferences` describes the first.
