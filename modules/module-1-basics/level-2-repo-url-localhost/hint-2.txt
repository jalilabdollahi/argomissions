Argo CD's repo-server is a pod inside the cluster. For that pod, `localhost` is the pod itself, not your laptop.
The repo is the same, but the cluster knows Gitea by its Service DNS name: service `gitea` in namespace `gitea`,
port 3000 (`gitea.gitea.svc.cluster.local`). Not every short name resolves from the `argocd` namespace.
