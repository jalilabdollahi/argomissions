One controller rewrites one label on every Deployment in the cluster. Argo CD can be told to ignore a field
per Application (spec.ignoreDifferences), but you would have to repeat that for every present and future
Application. The same setting exists once for the whole Argo CD installation, in the argocd-cm ConfigMap,
under keys that start with `resource.customizations.ignoreDifferences.` (operator manual, "Diffing Customization").
