Key: resource.customizations.actions.<group>_<Kind>, here apps_Deployment. Its value is YAML with
  discovery.lua   returns a table: action name -> {disabled = true|false}   (obj is the live Deployment)
  definitions     a list of {name, action.lua}; action.lua modifies obj and returns it
Remember that annotations may be nil in Lua. Two actions are needed: maintenance-on and maintenance-off, and the
list must reflect the current state of the Deployment.
