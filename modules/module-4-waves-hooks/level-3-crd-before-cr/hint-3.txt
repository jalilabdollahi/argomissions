Install the CRD through its own Application first (do not kubectl apply it, Argo CD must own it), wait until it
is Healthy, then sync the instances again:
  argocd app sync sensor-crds
  argocd app sync sensor-hub
(For installs from scratch, teams order this in Git: sync waves on the child Applications of an app of apps, or
SkipDryRunOnMissingResource=true plus retries on the instances.)
